About our rooms

Six room types, park or sea.

Villa Maria has six room types, from Standard rooms to the Master SPA Suite. Every room faces the park or the Adriatic and is a quiet place to sleep well, with a writing desk, power by the bed and high-speed Wi-Fi.

Standard and Superior rooms

Comfortable rooms with a garden or partial sea view and a writing desk. The Superior Room is ideal for one or two nights and is the standard room of the Executive Business Stay Package.

Deluxe rooms

More space, Adriatic views, a desk and a lounge area. Our choice for stays of three nights or more.

Junior Suites

A separate sitting area and room to spread out, good for families and longer stays. Some open onto a terrace with loungers and a view of the sea.

Master SPA Suite

Our most special room: a private terrace with a hot tub and a view over the Adriatic, for anniversaries, honeymoons or simply a few days just for you.

Families and pets

With the Family Discount, family rooms and suites sleep 2 adults and 2 teens, with breakfast and spa included. Pets are welcome for a supplement. Tell us who is coming and we will suggest the best room.

Little touches

Share your preferences once (pillow, a quiet floor, a room near the lift or away from it) and we keep them for your next stay. A late check-out can often be arranged on request.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

Which room is right for me?

The Superior Room, with a garden or partial sea view and a desk, suits short stays of 1 to 2 nights. The Deluxe Room and the Suites have more space and Adriatic views, for 3 nights or more.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Your check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation. If you need an early check-in or a late check-out, ask us and we will do our best.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome for a supplement. Tell us when you book so we can prepare the right room.

Is there parking?

Yes. Private parking is free for guests and includes EV charging.

What is the cancellation policy?

If you book your next stay during your visit (10% off), you can cancel for free up to 6 months before arrival and change dates up to 6 weeks before. For other bookings, the terms are in your booking confirmation.

Do I pay when I send a booking request?

No. You choose your dates, package and room, and we reply by email with the price and confirmation.
