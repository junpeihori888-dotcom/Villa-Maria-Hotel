About our rooms

Every kind of stay, park or sea.

Villa Maria has rooms for every kind of stay, from a Standard room for a short visit to the Master SPA Suite with its own sauna. Rooms face the park or the Adriatic, and most are in the central building with direct access to the Linfa spa from your floor.

Standard rooms

A simple, functional room with a park view, in a quiet part of the hotel reached along the corridor past the winter garden. It is not directly connected to the spa: you reach it through reception. Ideal for a short stay on a budget.

Superior rooms

In the main building near the spa, with direct access from your floor and a view of the pine trees in the park or of the sea in the distance.

Deluxe rooms

In the central building with direct spa access. Most have a terrace looking at the sea or the pine forest, and each comes with a double bed or two single beds.

Junior Suites

In the central building, with a sleeping area and a separate sitting area, a terrace over the sea or the park, and direct access to the spa by lift.

Family Rooms

The ideal solution for families who want total privacy and maximum comfort. Tell us who is coming and we will suggest the right layout.

Mini SPA and Master SPA Suites

The Mini SPA Suite has a hydromassage tub for two inside the room and a sea-view terrace with a solarium. The Master SPA Suite has two rooms, a sauna for two and a large corner terrace with a solarium and a heated mini jacuzzi, facing east and fully private.

The Cottage, for guests with pets

A small annex at the hotel entrance with its own entrance, about 39 m², with a fully fenced outdoor area for pets of all sizes. Inside: air conditioning, a coffee machine, a kitchenette with microwave, LCD TV, Wi-Fi, an iron and board and a park view. Laundry service on request.

In every room

Free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, satellite TV, a safe, a hairdryer, a wellness kit and spa access. The minibar is charged.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

Which room is right for me?

For a short stay on a budget, the Standard Room faces the park. The Superior Room, near the spa with a pine grove or distant sea view, suits 1 to 2 nights. Deluxe rooms and Junior Suites have terraces over the sea or the park, Family Rooms suit families, and the Mini SPA and Master SPA Suites have their own spa in the room.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Your check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation. If you need an early check-in or a late check-out, ask us and we will do our best.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets of all sizes are welcome for a supplement. The Cottage, at the hotel entrance, has a fully fenced outdoor area for them. Tell us when you book so we can prepare the right room.

Is there parking?

Yes. Outdoor parking is free for guests and has charging points for electric cars.

What is the cancellation policy?

If you book your next stay during your visit (10% off), you can cancel for free up to 6 months before arrival and change dates up to 6 weeks before. For other bookings, the terms are in your booking confirmation.

Do I pay when I send a booking request?

No. You choose your dates, package and room, and we reply by email with the price and confirmation.
