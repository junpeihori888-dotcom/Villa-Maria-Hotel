A Letter to the Family Planner

Fourteen tabs open at 11 PM? Rooms, meals, cancellation and getting here, told the way a friend would tell you.

3 min read · English

It's 11 PM. The house is finally quiet. You have fourteen browser tabs open, a half-finished cup of tea, and a spreadsheet that would impress your colleagues at work. You're planning the family trip again.

We see you. And this one's for you.

Choosing a hotel you've never stayed at, in a region you've never visited, is a leap of faith. You can't walk in and look around. You rely on photos, reviews, and a gut feeling. So here's everything we'd want to know if we were in your shoes, told the way a friend would tell you over coffee.

Finding the right room

Villa Maria has 87 rooms, which means it's big enough to have real choice but small enough to still feel personal. If you're a family of four, the Family Suites are the easy answer, and the team can add cots and bed rails if you have little ones.

If this trip is also a bit of a reward for the grown-ups (and it should be), look at the Superior rooms. They have views over the pine park or toward the sea, and they connect directly to the spa from their floor. For something truly special, the Mini SPA Suite has a jetted tub, and the Master SPA Suite has its own sauna and a sea view. Every room has free Wi-Fi, a minibar and a safe.

The meal plan question

People sometimes search for an all-inclusive hotel near Pescara, and we understand why. When you're travelling with kids, you want fewer decisions, not more. Villa Maria isn't all-inclusive, but Half Board comes close: breakfast and one main meal each day at the hotel restaurant, with Abruzzese and international dishes, plus a kids' menu for the little ones.

Honestly, after a beach day, not having to find a restaurant that seats four, has a children's menu, and isn't fully booked is worth a lot.

Booking with peace of mind

Most families book through sites they know, and that's completely fair. Reviews matter, and Villa Maria's are strong. But it's worth checking the hotel's own website too. When you book direct, it's simply easier to talk to the team, ask for a quiet room or a cot, and add spa treatments or padel to your stay.

Before you click "pay," take a moment with the cancellation terms. Flexible rates usually let you cancel for free up to a set date, while non-refundable rates are cheaper but locked in. Also good to know: if you leave earlier than planned, the hotel may charge one night.

Getting there without the headache

Here's the good news: Villa Maria is one of the easiest hotels near Pescara Airport, just about ten minutes by car. You can rent a car at the airport or ask the hotel to help arrange a transfer. Driving down the coast? The A14 motorway exit is closed, and there are 100 free parking spaces waiting. Coming from Rome? It's around two and a half hours across the country, a beautiful drive through the mountains.

Check-in starts at 2 PM. If your flight lands early, ask about early check-in; it's available for a fee when rooms are ready. And if you're arriving late at night, just let the team know.

The small things that make a big difference

Before you leave home, reserve your free two-hour spa slot and book padel if the kids want to play. In July and August, these fill up fast. It takes five minutes and saves you a lot of disappointment later.

After the trip

Holidays have a funny way of following you home. Maybe you need an invoice for your records, or you realise your daughter's favourite hoodie is still hanging behind the bathroom door. Don't worry; just email the front desk. The team can send documents and help with lost items.

And if your stay was everything you hoped, leave a review. You know better than anyone how much those reviews helped you decide. Your words will help the next planner, sitting up at 11 PM with fourteen tabs open.

Coming back next summer

Here's something we've noticed: families who come in the summer often come back. Once you've found a place where everyone is happy, why start the search all over again?

When you rebook, tell the team which room you loved, and ask about any perks for returning guests. Follow @hotel_villamaria on Instagram for seasonal offers, including couples' spa packages. Next year's planning might just take one tab instead of fourteen.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

Is Villa Maria all-inclusive?

No, but Half Board includes breakfast and one main meal per day, which keeps things simple for families.

How far is it from Pescara Airport?

About 10 minutes by car.

Can the hotel arrange an airport transfer?

Yes, contact the hotel before your arrival to arrange one. Taxis and rental cars are also available at the airport.

Can I cancel for free?

It depends on your rate. Flexible rates usually allow free cancellation up to a certain date.

Why book directly with the hotel?

It's easier to add spa treatments, padel, and special requests like cots or room preferences.

What if we leave early?

The hotel may charge one night as a penalty for early departure.

How do I get a copy of my invoice?

Email or call the front desk with your booking details.

What if we forgot something?

Contact the hotel as soon as possible. The team can check lost property and help with shipping.

Can we request the same room next year?

Yes, just mention it when you rebook.

Are there perks for returning guests?

Ask the team when you rebook. Returning guests may be offered seasonal deals or upgrades.
