Things to do

Water, wellness and the open air.

Days at Villa Maria can be as busy or as slow as you like: two pools in a 6-hectare park, the beach by shuttle, the Linfa spa, padel and an outdoor gym, a Kids Club in summer, and the hills, coast and towns of Abruzzo all within easy reach.

Swimming pools

Two outdoor pools in the park, among pines and olive trees. The Blue Pool, up to 2.5 metres deep, is for a real swim and is open until 15 September. The Riviera Pool is made for children: no deeper than 50 cm, with a twelve-jet fountain, a water umbrella and hydromassage points, open until 30 September.

The beach

A free shuttle takes you to a white-sand beach in Francavilla al Mare, at a private lido with a special agreement with the hotel. The lido has a bar and a restaurant for lunches on the beach, aperitifs and evening events.

Kids Club

In July and August: a play room with games, books and puzzles, the Riviera Pool, educational workshops from Tuesday to Saturday and an outdoor play area.

Linfa wellness & spa

Indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, saunas, a Turkish bath and emotional showers. Hotel guests enjoy a free two-hour spa session; booking is required.

Padel

The new padel court is in the park, between the two pools. Book a slot for a family match or bring a client: Beyond the Meeting Room pairs the game with the spa, an aperitivo and dinner.

Outdoor gym and the park

An outdoor fitness area with Technogym equipment among the trees, with a view of the sea, and 6 hectares of park to walk or jog in.

Cycling the coast

The Bike to Coast cycle and pedestrian path runs along the Abruzzo coast towards the Costa dei Trabocchi, a few minutes from the hotel.

Abruzzo Experience

Guided excursions with transfers, also in English and German: the Majella and Gran Sasso national parks, the trabocchi coast, Chieti and Pescara. Ask us what is running during your stay.

Days out with children

Two favourites with families: the Guardiagrele adventure park, with tree-top trails in the woods at the foot of the Majella, and the zoo in Lanciano.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

What pools does the hotel have?

Two outdoor pools in the park: the Blue Pool, up to 2.5 m deep (open until 15 September), and the Riviera Pool for children, no deeper than 50 cm, with a twelve-jet fountain and a water umbrella (open until 30 September). The Linfa spa has indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage all year.

Is there a beach?

Yes. A free shuttle takes you to a white-sand beach in Francavilla al Mare, a few kilometres away, at a private lido with a bar and restaurant that has an agreement with the hotel.

What is in the Linfa wellness & spa?

Indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, a whirlpool, saunas, a Turkish bath, chromotherapy and aromatherapy showers, a herbal tea corner and a private garden. Every hotel guest has a free two-hour session; booking is required (+39 085 45 00 55 60). Massages for one or two can be added.

What is there for children?

In July and August the Kids Club has a play room with games, books and puzzles, educational workshops from Tuesday to Saturday and an outdoor play area. There is also the Riviera Pool for children, the park, padel and the beach by shuttle. Nearby, families love the Guardiagrele adventure park and the zoo in Lanciano.

What can we do nearby?

Cycle the Bike to Coast path towards the Costa dei Trabocchi, a few minutes from the hotel, walk in the Pineta Dannunziana nature reserve in Pescara, or join a guided excursion to the Majella and Gran Sasso national parks, Chieti and Pescara.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets of all sizes are welcome for a supplement. The Cottage, at the hotel entrance, has a fully fenced outdoor area for them. Tell us when you book so we can prepare the right room.
