About the restaurant

Abruzzo at every meal.

The restaurant looks out over the hotel’s centuries-old park, with a sea-view terrace for summer evenings and events. The kitchen cooks Italian dishes and Abruzzo specialities with local products, from breakfast to dinner.

Opening times

Breakfast 7:00–10:00 on the ground floor, and on the terrace in the warmer months. Lunch 12:30–14:00, dinner 19:30–22:00. The bar is open from 10:00 to 23:00. Times can change with the season.

Breakfast

A buffet with fresh fruit, cakes, pastries and savoury dishes, included in our packages. Business guests can have it early, before the first call.

Lunch and dinner

À la carte Italian cuisine and Abruzzo specialities, served in the dining room or on the terrace. Long family dinners and quiet business dinners both have their place.

The chef’s table

For a special evening, or the dinner after a padel match, the chef’s table is where conversations last and deals are made.

The terrace and events

The sea-view terrace hosts aperitifs, celebrations and events. Ask us about a private dinner or a party for your group.

The beach and the trabocchi

Our partner lido on the beach has its own bar and restaurant for lunches on the sand and summer evenings. Further south, along the Costa dei Trabocchi, the old fishing platforms over the sea serve fish dishes such as brodetto: ask us to book a table.

La Bottega di Villa Maria

Our corner of local products and Abruzzo flavours, to taste during your stay and take a little of the region home.

Allergies and reservations

Tell us about allergies or diets when you book, or send us a message. To reserve a table, call +39 085 45 00 51.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included in our packages and served as a buffet from 7:00 to 10:00, on the terrace in the warmer months. Lunch is 12:30–14:00 and dinner 19:30–22:00.

Where is Villa Maria Hotel & Spa?

On the hill above Francavilla al Mare, in Abruzzo, 5 km from the centre of Pescara. Chieti, Pescara and Abruzzo International Airport are about 15 minutes away, Pescara Centrale station has easy rail connections, and the A14 Pescara Sud exit is close by.

Is there parking?

Yes. Outdoor parking is free for guests and has charging points for electric cars.

Why book directly with the hotel?

You get the package exactly as designed, a real person before and during your stay, and extras that are not on booking sites, such as the €20 in-house voucher on your first direct Family Discount booking.
