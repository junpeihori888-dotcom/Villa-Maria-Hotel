About the Linfa spa

Your own space to unwind.

Linfa is the hotel’s wellness centre. Its relaxation area has indoor and outdoor pools with several hydromassage points, a whirlpool, saunas, a Turkish bath, chromotherapy and aromatherapy showers with water jets and a waterfall, a herbal tea corner and a private garden. Every hotel guest gets a free two-hour session; booking is required.

The relaxation area

A visit lasts two hours and includes the indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, the whirlpool, the saunas, the Turkish bath, the emotional showers, the herbal tea corner and the private garden.

Your free two-hour session

Every hotel guest can enjoy two hours in the relaxation area at no extra cost. Book your time at reception, call the spa on +39 085 45 00 55 60 or write to benessere@hvillamaria.it, especially in high season.

Opening hours

Monday to Thursday 10:00–21:00, Friday 10:00–23:30, Saturday 9:00–23:30, Sunday 9:00–21:00. Hours can change with the season, so check with the spa before you go.

Massages and treatments

Massages and beauty treatments for one or for two. Couple massages take place at the same time in two separate cabins. Wellness paths combine the spa with a massage, such as a volcanic hot-stone back massage.

Private SPA for two

A private spa in the Master SPA Suite: a sauna for two, a heated mini jacuzzi on the terrace and a solarium over the sea. Some packages add a couple massage and an aperitif on the sea-view terrace.

Fitness

An outdoor fitness area with Technogym equipment among the trees of the park, with a view of the sea, and the padel court between the two pools.

Spa in your package

Spa access is included in the Family Discount and in the Executive Business Stay Package. Beyond the Meeting Room ends the match with sauna, spa and a massage.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

What is in the Linfa wellness & spa?

Indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, a whirlpool, saunas, a Turkish bath, chromotherapy and aromatherapy showers, a herbal tea corner and a private garden. Every hotel guest has a free two-hour session; booking is required (+39 085 45 00 55 60). Massages for one or two can be added.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Your check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation. If you need an early check-in or a late check-out, ask us and we will do our best.

Is there parking?

Yes. Outdoor parking is free for guests and has charging points for electric cars.

Why book directly with the hotel?

You get the package exactly as designed, a real person before and during your stay, and extras that are not on booking sites, such as the €20 in-house voucher on your first direct Family Discount booking.
