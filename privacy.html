Privacy & cookies.

This page explains what this website does with your data.

Data we collect

We only collect data when you send it with a form: a booking request (name, email, phone, dates, guests, room, company and requests if given), a message, a review (with your booking reference, so we can check that you stayed with us), a community sign-up, or a colleague referral (your colleague's name and email).

Why and for how long

We use them only to answer your request, manage your stay and, if you sign up, send you our news. We do not sell them or share them for advertising. We keep them only as long as needed or required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or unsubscribe, at any time by writing to us. You can also contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. It only saves a few small preferences in your browser: whether you have already seen the newsletter sign-up, your coupon code and the type of stay you looked at. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's IP address.

External links

The Google Maps link leads to a site with its own privacy rules.
