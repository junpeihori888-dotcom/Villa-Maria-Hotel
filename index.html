About Villa Maria

A four-star hotel & spa above the Adriatic.

Villa Maria Hotel & Spa is a four-star hotel on the hills of Francavilla al Mare, 5 km from the centre of Pescara. It sits in a 6-hectare park of pines and olive trees with a view over the Adriatic coast, about 15 minutes from Chieti, Pescara and Abruzzo International Airport. Two outdoor pools, the Linfa spa, a padel court and an outdoor gym are inside the park, and a free shuttle takes you to a white-sand beach.

The hotel and its park

A 6-hectare park to walk or jog in, among pines and olive groves, with the Adriatic coast in view from the hill. Inside it are the two outdoor pools, the new padel court between the pools and an outdoor fitness area with Technogym equipment.

Rooms and suites

Standard, Superior and Deluxe rooms, Junior Suites, Family Rooms, the Mini SPA Suite and the Master SPA Suite, plus the Cottage for guests travelling with a pet. Every room has free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, satellite TV, a safe, a hairdryer, a wellness kit and spa access.

For families

In July and August the Kids Club has a play room with games, books and puzzles, educational workshops from Tuesday to Saturday and an outdoor play area. The Riviera Pool is made for children: no deeper than 50 cm, with a twelve-jet fountain and a water umbrella. Family Rooms give everyone privacy and comfort.

For business travel

Five conference rooms, from small meetings to plenary sessions, with video-conferencing and fast Wi-Fi, 15 minutes from Abruzzo Airport, Chieti and Pescara. The day can end in the spa, in the outdoor gym or on the padel court.

Wellness and food

The Linfa wellness centre has indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, saunas, a Turkish bath, emotional showers, a herbal tea corner and a private garden, with a free two-hour session for hotel guests. Breakfast is served from 7:00 to 10:00, lunch from 12:30 to 14:00 and dinner from 19:30 to 22:00.

Pets welcome

Pets of all sizes are welcome. The Cottage, at the hotel entrance with its own door, has a fully fenced outdoor area just for them. A supplement applies: tell us when you book.

Questions & answers

Good to know before you go.

Where is Villa Maria Hotel & Spa?

On the hill above Francavilla al Mare, in Abruzzo, 5 km from the centre of Pescara. Chieti, Pescara and Abruzzo International Airport are about 15 minutes away, Pescara Centrale station has easy rail connections, and the A14 Pescara Sud exit is close by.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Your check-in and check-out times are in your booking confirmation. If you need an early check-in or a late check-out, ask us and we will do our best.

Is there parking?

Yes. Outdoor parking is free for guests and has charging points for electric cars.

What is in the Linfa wellness & spa?

Indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage points, a whirlpool, saunas, a Turkish bath, chromotherapy and aromatherapy showers, a herbal tea corner and a private garden. Every hotel guest has a free two-hour session; booking is required (+39 085 45 00 55 60). Massages for one or two can be added.

What pools does the hotel have?

Two outdoor pools in the park: the Blue Pool, up to 2.5 m deep (open until 15 September), and the Riviera Pool for children, no deeper than 50 cm, with a twelve-jet fountain and a water umbrella (open until 30 September). The Linfa spa has indoor and outdoor pools with hydromassage all year.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets of all sizes are welcome for a supplement. The Cottage, at the hotel entrance, has a fully fenced outdoor area for them. Tell us when you book so we can prepare the right room.

Why book directly with the hotel?

You get the package exactly as designed, a real person before and during your stay, and extras that are not on booking sites, such as the €20 in-house voucher on your first direct Family Discount booking.

What is the cancellation policy?

If you book your next stay during your visit (10% off), you can cancel for free up to 6 months before arrival and change dates up to 6 weeks before. For other bookings, the terms are in your booking confirmation.
